Sun

All year, the nine panels record the sun on their walls. Every second Monday someone walks the route and collects the record. Each week becomes an edition: 63 dials, nine panels by seven days.

Over the years the editions become a portrait of the sun in nine streets of Bosa. Short winter days draw short arcs; summer fills the half circle. The panels measure the sunlight reaching each wall, not electricity: the headline figure is hours of direct sun.

How the record is made

Collected on foot, published every two weeks

A week is complete once Sunday's sunset has passed, so each walk brings back two complete weeks: two new editions. The panels have no internet, so nothing here is live; the newest edition is up to two weeks old.

Every week gets a number, with no gaps. A week a panel did not wake shows its outline, like any day it missed.

These pages are the record. The only artworks are the prints made from each summer event, La Meridiana.
